Open Health

Your sleep, HRV, resting heart rate and training load show in the Health app. COROS has no daily recovery score, so Health shows recovery from another source or none. This app only keeps the COROS sign-in and the synced copy.

Connect your COROS in two steps. You sign in on COROS's own page; nothing to copy or paste.

  1. You need the free COROS account your watch syncs to in the COROS app. There is no fee, no developer account and no approval: COROS lets apps like this one connect directly.

    Sleep, HRV and resting heart rate come from COROS watches that measure them. No account yet? Get the COROS app.

    1. Tap Connect with COROS. COROS's sign-in page opens in a new tab.
    2. Sign in with your COROS email and password and allow access.
    3. Come back here. This screen finishes by itself within a few seconds.
    Open COROS sign-in

    The sign-in page works for about five minutes. If it runs out, tap Connect with COROS again. Pick a region only if Automatic does not find your account.

Disconnect Coros?

The app ends its COROS sign-in and deletes its synced copy. Health stops showing COROS data. Your data on COROS is not touched.
